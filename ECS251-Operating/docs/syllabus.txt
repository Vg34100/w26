 ECS251 - Operating
ECS 251 Operating Systems (Winter 2026)

ECS 251 will introduce students to research in Operating Systems (OS). We will read a series of foundational and cutting-edge research papers exploring topics of OS design, scheduling, memory management, file systems, and concurrency. We will improve skills in critical thinking, creativity, presentations, and systems programming. The class format will consist of lectures, paper discussions, group meetings, and project presentations.

Prerequisites

This class builds on the undergraduate operating systems course, such as the ECS 150 course at UC Davis. We will dive deeply into topics of kernel design, scheduling, memory management, file systems, and concurrency.
Readings

Course material will be conveyed via a series of systems research papers. Students will lead a presentation of a research paper and seed a discussion. Optional readings of an Operating Systems textbook, such as Operating Systems: Three Easy Pieces

Links to an external site., are recommended to brush up on the necessary background to understand the research papers.
Quizzes

There will be 8 quizzes worth 4% each throughout the quarter. I will replace your lowest quiz grade for the quarter with full credit.
Lead a Lecture

You will present one research paper to the class and lead a discussion. Presentations will be done in groups of 3-4 students. Groups must share their lecture slides and other discussion materials with the instructor by noon the day before the lecture. More information on leading a lecture can be found here
Research Project

You will design and carry out a quarter-long systems research project. This project will be done in teams of 3-4 students. More information on projects can be found here
Late Policy

Unless otherwise stated, late assignments will receive an automatic 50% deduction. Late final project deliverables will not be accepted. Please inform the instructor if you are having difficulties meeting the deadlines of this class.
