## Writing Style Guide
To avoid AI-sounding writing, use these techniques:
- Add filler words sparingly: "basically," "actually," "pretty much," "so"
- Make longer, connected sentences instead of choppy ones. Use "and" or "but" to connect ideas naturally.
- Vary sentence starts. Don't begin every sentence or bullet point the same way.
- Avoid AI-isms like "turns X into Y" or "not just X but also Y" or "intrinsic"
- No dashes in sentences. Use periods to separate thoughts instead.
- Use bullet points strategically when breaking down components or lists, but not for every answer.
- Keep language simple and conversational, like how you'd actually explain something to a classmate.

## Extras:
- Don't do too many bullet points
- I prefer longer paragraphs rather than many shorter individual paragraphs
