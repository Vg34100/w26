Famous Speeches (choose one, or pick your own multi-paragraph text)

    Tech & Science

Ada Lovelace: first computer programmer, first published algorithm, early vision of general-purpose computing, pioneering woman in tech.

Claude Shannon: father of the digital age, inventor of the 'bit', founder of information theory, pioneer of logic gates and AI.

Elon Musk: Built SpaceX into a reusable-rocketry powerhouse, helped mainstream electric vehicles with Tesla, and expanded global satellite internet via Starlink.

Fei-Fei Li: ImageNet leader who catalyzed modern computer vision and deep learning, prominent advocate of human-centered AI, co-founder of AI4ALL.

Mark Zuckerberg: Co-founded Facebook and scaled it into a defining global social platform, later steering Meta’s bets on AI and immersive computing.

Steve Jobs: Apple co-founder who drove iconic product revolutions (Mac, iPod, iPhone) and set a new standard for design-led consumer technology.

Sheryl Sandberg: Operational powerhouse at Google and Facebook/Meta, key architect of modern digital advertising scale, shaping leadership discourse about women in tech.

    Politics & Government

Aung Sang Suu Kyi: Nobel Peace Prize–winning pro-democracy leader, emblem of resistance during years of house arrest, led Myanmar’s National League for Democracy to landmark electoral victories.

Barack Obama: 44th U.S. President, first Black president, passed the Affordable Care Act, and advanced major climate and diplomatic initiatives.

Donald Trump: Real-estate and media mogul, who had six companies declare bankruptcy, turned 45th and 47th U.S. President, reshaped the federal judiciary, enacted major tax reforms, and is credited with brokering the Abraham Accords in the Middle East.

Hillary Clinton: Former First Lady, U.S. Senator, and Secretary of State; first woman to win a major-party U.S. presidential nomination; major figure in modern U.S. policy and diplomacy.

    Social Justice & Human Rights

Angela Davis: Influential scholar-activist and author, major voice in Black feminist thought and prison abolition, shaping modern justice movements.

Mahatma Gandhi: Architect of mass nonviolent resistance (satyagraha), pivotal leader in India’s independence movement, global model for civil rights strategy.

Malala Yousafzai: Youngest-ever Nobel Peace Prize laureate, global champion for girls’ education, founder of the Malala Fund.

Martin Luther King Jr: Defining U.S. civil-rights leader, champion of nonviolent activism, Nobel Peace Prize laureate, helped catalyze landmark civil-rights legislation.

    Arts & Entertainment

J. K. Rowling: Creator of Harry Potter, one of history’s most successful book franchises, transforming children’s publishing into a global cultural phenomenon.

Oprah Winfrey: "Queen of All Media", often ranked as the most influential woman in the world, objectively the richest Black woman and the wealthiest female celebrity.

Meryl Streep: Acting benchmark with record-setting major-award nominations, acclaimed for unparalleled range and decades of leading performances.
