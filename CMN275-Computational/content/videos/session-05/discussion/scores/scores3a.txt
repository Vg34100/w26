## Overall Assessment

### Groundedness Profile
Elon Musk's 2017 IAC speech scores **highly grounded** (3.5/5.0) with a distinct profile:

**Strengths (High Groundedness):**
1. **Lexical concreteness**: Extensive use of tangible objects, physical materials, and measurable specifications
2. **Specificity**: Proper nouns, brand names, and detailed technical specifications anchor the narrative in reality
3. **Structural clarity**: Active voice with explicit agents and actions ("We build," "It lands," "You can transfer")
4. **Low nominalization**: Preserves dynamic verbs rather than hiding processes in abstract nouns

**Moderate Groundedness:**
5. **Sensorimotor engagement**: Strong visual and action modalities but limited tactile/olfactory/gustatory depth
6. **Jargon density**: Technical vocabulary requires aerospace domain knowledge, reducing accessibility

- **Technical precision** (jargon, specifications)
- **Physical reality** (concrete objects, active verbs)
- **Inspirational abstraction** (future, civilization, multiplanetary)

The 3.5/5.0 score reflects a text deeply anchored in physical engineering realities (rockets, engines, propellant) while operating in a technical domain that inherently requires specialized vocabulary. Unlike purely abstract corporate or academic discourse (which might score 0-2), this speech "touches grass" through constant reference to buildable, testable, physical systems.
