Example 1: Session 4 (AI) - Podcast Fact-Check

I listened to the AI podcast and they mentioned you can reduce bias by a factor of 10 by removing gender/race data, with accuracy only dropping from 85.3% to 84.7%. I verified with the chat that these numbers were correct, but then I asked if the machine still finds other ways to figure out race/gender even when you remove those attributes and it looks like the podcast skipped over something like this entirely. The AI explained that even without explicit race labels, names like "Jerome" vs "Harry" end up clustering differently in the data based on historical patterns. So the machine still builds in bias, it just does it through other variables instead of the direct ones.
