Agreements:
Both ChatGPT and Gemini basically agreed on a lot of the main patterns which was interesting. Both of them scored Trump's anger at exactly 0.7, and Gandhi's anger way lower at 0.2 for ChatGPT and 0.1 for Gemini. This makes sense to me because Trump's tweets were full of direct attacks like "Crooked Hillary" and "Fake News Media" etc., while Gandhi was trying to be more, diplomatic(?), even though he was still defiant. Both LLMs also gave really high defiance scores to both speakers. Trump got 0.9 and Gandhi got either 0.8 or 1.0 depending on which model, which shows they were both clearly resisting something, just in different ways. And both models gave Trump way higher disgust scores (0.6-0.8) compared to Gandhi (0.1), probably because of Trump's language like "pile of garbage" and calling things "disgraceful" all the time.

Disagreements:
The biggest disagreement I noticed was Gandhi's fear score. ChatGPT gave him 0.4 which is moderate fear, but Gemini gave him exactly 0.0, so no fear at all. I think this happened because ChatGPT probably saw phrases like "receive bullets on our chest" and "warfare and bloodshed" and interpreted that as some level of fear being expressed, but Gemini seemed to think Gandhi was just warning people about danger without actually being afraid himself? Another big difference was Gandhi's defiance score where Gemini gave a perfect 1.0 because of the whole "Do or Die" thing, but ChatGPT was more conservative and gave 0.8. For Trump's scores the differences were smaller, just minor variations in disgust (0.6 vs 0.8) and joy (0.5 vs 0.6).
