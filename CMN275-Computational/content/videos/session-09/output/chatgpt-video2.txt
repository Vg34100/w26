### **Anger: 0.04**

Very little anger appears in the discussion. The space is gentle and supportive, with almost no hostile or frustrated remarks.

### **Fear: 0.06**

A small amount of fear appears in comments about entering adulthood, feeling lost, or facing life challenges. These are mild and are quickly followed by reassurance or motivation.

### **Disgust: 0.03**

Disgust is nearly absent. The comment section is unusually kind, and several people even note the lack of rude comments.

### **Joy: 0.78**

Joy is strong throughout. People smile at small moments in the video, feel uplifted, and express love for Bernard as a guiding figure. Many say the video improved their outlook on life.

### **Hopefulness: 0.92**

Hope is the strongest emotion. The comments focus on motivation, guidance, future growth, and believing in oneself. Many describe the video as appearing exactly when needed, which reinforces a shared feeling of purpose and encouragement.
